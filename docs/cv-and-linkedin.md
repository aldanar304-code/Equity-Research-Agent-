## LinkedIn: Projects section
Title: AI Equity Research Agent

Description: An AI agent that does an equity analyst's groundwork. Given a ticker, it downloads the company's SEC filings (about 1,000 pages), researches 10 years of financials, competitors, analyst expectations, risks and executive pay, values the company with a DCF in bear/base/bull scenarios, and writes a fund-style investment memo with a Buy/Hold/Sell view, in English or Spanish. A second pass fact-checks the draft against its sources; across four memos it made 54 self-corrections. About $0.45 and 4 minutes per memo. Built with Python and the Claude API.

Skills: Python · Financial Analysis · Equity Research · DCF Valuation · Artificial Intelligence (AI) · Data Analysis

## LinkedIn: post
I built an AI equity research agent. 📈

My favourite part: after writing, it fact-checks itself. On Apple it caught that its own price-target math was wrong; on Amazon it noticed a debt ratio flattered by one-off gains. Across four memos it made 54 corrections to its own drafts.
