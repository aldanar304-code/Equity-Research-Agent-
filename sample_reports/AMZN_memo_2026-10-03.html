1. Recommendation and thesis

Hold. AWS is accelerating and operating income is rising fast. But the stock already prices that in at about 24x forward P/E, and trailing free cash flow is negative. My probability-weighted DCF is about $212 per share (my estimate), below the market price. The consensus target of about $331 needs the bull case to come true.

  1. AWS acceleration is real and is the core of the thesis. - AWS grew 37% in Q2 2026, after 28% in Q1 and 17% a year earlier. Segment operating income was $16.6B on $42.2B of sales, a margin of about 39% (8-K-EX-2026-07). - Testable: Q3 AWS growth stays at or above 30% and margin at or above 35%.
  2. The 2026 capex surge delays free cash flow. - Cash capex was $53.1B in Q2. Management raised 2026 capex guidance to about $220B from about $200B, citing memory costs (web: Webull recap of the Q2 call). - TTM free cash flow was an outflow of $7.6B (8-K-EX-2026-07). - Testable: TTM FCF stays negative through Q4 2026. A positive FCF inflection is unlikely before 2027 (my view).
  3. Reported earnings overstate run-rate profit. - Q2 net income of $62.6B ($5.75 per share) included $53.4B of pre-tax non-operating income, mostly Anthropic mark-ups (8-K-EX-2026-07). - Consensus FY26 EPS is $12.88, while FY27 is $10.51, an implied 18% decline (my arithmetic). That gap is consistent with the gains rolling off. - Testable: I expect Q3 operating income inside the $22.5-26.5B guidance range.
  4. Funding is becoming a balance-sheet issue. - Total debt rose from $153B (Dec 2025) to $210B (Mar 2026) (quarterly balance sheet).
