## 8. Key risks and mitigants

Mitigant: some purchase agreements are cancellable or adjustable, and guarantee exposure declines as OpenAI pays its lease payments. Signal: commitments growing faster than revenue.
3. **Customer concentration.** One to three direct customers each account for 13–21% of revenue (10-Q-2026-07; 10-Q-2026-04). The August 2026 SB Energy guarantees are tied to 20-year leases to OpenAI (8-K-EX-2026-08-2). Mitigant: the mix is broadening (ACIE up 138% year on year).

5. **Gross margin and memory costs.** The supply commitment increase is primarily for memory. Q3 gross margin is guided down to 74.0%. Signal: gross margin below 73%.
6. **Valuation and volatility.** Beta is 2.2, one-year volatility is 37.7% and the 1-year max drawdown was −20.2%. The stock trades at 29.6x trailing earnings.
