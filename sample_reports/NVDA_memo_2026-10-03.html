1. Recommendation and thesis

NVIDIA is operating at extraordinary scale and profitability, and our base-case DCF ($229) lands almost exactly on the current price. At 15x consensus forward EPS the stock is cheap if the AI build-out keeps compounding. But the balance sheet is taking on more supplier, customer-financing and guarantee exposure than it has in the past, so we rate it Hold with a modestly positive bias.

  1. Growth continues through FY28. Q3 FY27 guidance is $108B ±2% against $96.2B in Q2 (8-K-EX-2026-08). Test: Q3 revenue at or above $106B (the low end of the guide), and FY28 consensus revenue ($683B) holding up. Per CNBC, Huang is guiding to roughly 70% FY28 growth.
  2. Margins hold near 74-75% gross. Q3 is guided to 74.0% ±50bp, down from 75.0%, and analysts quoted by Kiplinger flag early memory cost inflation. Test: gross margin of at least 73.5% through the Vera Rubin ramp.
  3. Cash conversion is weakening and the balance sheet is becoming a financing tool. Q2 free cash flow was only $21.4B against $59.7B of GAAP net income. Days sales outstanding rose to 60 from 45, supply commitments jumped to $279B from $119B, and maximum guarantee exposure totals $108.5B (8-K-EX-2026-08-2). Test: DSO back below 50, and no guarantee draws.
  4. The market already prices much of the good news. The stock is up 24.2% over 1 year against 16.2% for SPY, and sits near its $237.88 52-week high. Consensus EPS has been revised up (section 7), so the stock needs more than beats-and-raises to re-rate (my view).

8. Key risks and mitigants

  1. AI capex digestion and customer financing. The company says many AI clouds and model makers are "growing faster than their balance sheets" can support (8-K-EX-2026-08-2). DSO rose to 60 days. Signal: DSO and AI-cloud credit quality.
  2. Large commitments and guarantees. Supply commitments are $279B, total commitments $366B, additional commitments $56B, and maximum guarantee exposure $108.5B (10-Q-2026-07; 8-K-EX-2026-08-2). Mitigant: some purchase agreements are cancellable or adjustable, and guarantee exposure declines as OpenAI pays its lease payments. Signal: commitments growing faster than revenue.
  3. Customer concentration. One to three direct customers each account for 13–21% of revenue (10-Q-2026-07; 10-Q-2026-04). The August 2026 SB Energy guarantees are tied to 20-year leases to OpenAI (8-K-EX-2026-08-2). Mitigant: the mix is broadening (ACIE up 138% year on year).
  4. Export controls and China. The company has said it has effectively been foreclosed from China data center compute (10-Q-2025-10). Guidance assumes zero China data center revenue, so there is upside optionality. Further rules could limit sales elsewhere (10-Q-2026-07).
  5. Gross margin and memory costs. The supply commitment increase is primarily for memory. Q3 gross margin is guided down to 74.0%. Signal: gross margin below 73%.
  6. Valuation and volatility. Beta is 2.2, one-year volatility is 37.7% and the 1-year max drawdown was −20.2%. The stock trades at 29.6x trailing earnings.
  7. Governance and insider selling. Sales were $1.42B with no buying, mostly from one director. This is a minor signal.
